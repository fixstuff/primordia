      Key

      Green: sugar (energy). Blue: mineral (material for children). Each organism is coloured by lineage, brighter with more energy. Up close (zoom in) it is drawn from its genome: tails are flagella, the fringe is cilia, dark notches at the front are mouths, antennae are chemo-sensors (green tip: sugar, blue: mineral, yellow: smells other organisms), pale blobs inside are vacuoles. Combat: mandibles are jaws, a gold barb is a stinger, a tan rim is a shell, white points are spikes, a green dashed ring is toxin, and a faint body is camouflaged.
